## 1. Information we collect

QuizConquest is designed to collect as little personal information as possible. The only data the app processes is what is strictly necessary for gameplay.

### 1.1 Information you enter

- **Display name.** When you set up a match you enter a nickname. This name is stored only on your device and, during an online match, transmitted to the other player and to Unity's matchmaking service so it can appear in the opponent's UI. We do not require this to be your real name.
- **Avatar selection.** You choose an avatar icon from a built-in list. The selected index (a number) is shared with the other player during an online match.

We do **not** collect: email address, phone number, real name, age, gender, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, files, or advertising ID.

### 1.2 Device and other identifiers (anonymous Unity player ID and installation ID)

**A device/installation identifier and an anonymous player ID are sent to Unity Technologies.**

Every time you launch the game with an internet connection, the app signs in anonymously to **Unity Gaming Services** (Unity Technologies). This happens automatically at launch, **in every game mode** (including single-player, hot-seat, and Daily Challenge), and involves two identifiers:

- **Installation ID**: a random identifier generated by the Unity Services SDK for this installation of the app. It identifies the app install, not you personally, and it is not your Android ID, IMEI, serial number, or advertising ID.
- **Anonymous player ID**: a random identifier that Unity Authentication creates for your install the first time you sign in. It is not linked to your name, email, or phone number.

Both identifiers persist on that install and are reused on each launch. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data discards them, and a future install gets new ones.

These identifiers are used for **app functionality** (signing in, online matchmaking in section 1.3, and the competitive leaderboard in section 1.4), **account management** (keeping your rating tied to your install), and **fraud prevention and security** (verifying that both players report the same ranked-match result before a rating changes). They are not used for advertising, profiling, or tracking you across other apps.

Unity Technologies processes these identifiers on our behalf as a service provider. Its handling of them is governed by Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

### 1.2a Technical and diagnostic data sent to Unity

When the Unity Services SDK communicates with Unity's servers, it also sends basic technical data that comes with every request: your device's **IP address**, the app version, the Unity SDK version, the platform (Android), and limited SDK diagnostic/performance information (for example, whether a service call succeeded or failed). Unity uses this to operate and keep its services reliable. We do not use it to identify you.

### 1.3 Information collected during online matches

When you play an online (multiplayer) match, the following technical data is also processed by **Unity Gaming Services**, together with your anonymous player ID, so that two devices can find and talk to each other:

- The **IP address** of your device, used to route gameplay traffic through Unity Relay servers. Unity does not expose your IP to the other player.
- **Lobby and match metadata** (lobby ID, join code, host/client role, the display name and avatar index you entered for the current match). This is deleted shortly after the match ends.

We do not operate our own servers. The above is handled by Unity.

### 1.4 Competitive leaderboard (online ranked matches)

The game includes an optional competitive rank ladder. When you finish an **online** match, your updated skill rating (an ELO number) is stored on Unity's Leaderboards service against your anonymous player ID, together with the **display name** you chose for the match. Unlike the lobby/relay data above, this leaderboard entry **persists** so the ladder can show standings over time.

- Your display name on the leaderboard is **visible to other players** who view the ladder. Because of this, please do not enter your real name or any personal information as your display name — use a nickname.
- Only your anonymous player ID, rating, display name, and minimal match-result data (a match ID, your opponent's anonymous player ID, and win/loss/draw) are sent. The match-result data is used only to verify results and calculate ratings. No quiz answers, contacts, location, or other personal data are sent.
- Single-player, hot-seat, and Daily Challenge results are **not** submitted to the leaderboard; they stay on your device.

This data is stored by Unity Gaming Services (Leaderboards and Cloud Code), governed by Unity's privacy policy linked above. We do not operate our own servers and keep no separate copy.

### 1.5 Local data

Match settings (volume, last-used avatar, tutorial-hint flags) and your local progress (Knowledge Rank, match history, Daily Challenge streak, question history) are stored on your device via Android's local app storage (`PlayerPrefs` and local files). This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app removes it.

If you use the **Share** button on the results screen, the app creates a screenshot of the results, saves a copy to your device's Pictures folder, and opens Android's share sheet. The image is sent only to the app you choose to share it with. We never receive it.

### 1.6 In-app purchases

The app contains Unity In-App Purchasing, which connects to **Google Play Billing** when the app starts. All payments are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We never receive your payment card or billing details. If you make a purchase, the app receives only the purchase confirmation (product ID and receipt) needed to deliver the item.

### 1.7 We do **not** use

- Advertising SDKs or your advertising ID.
- Third-party analytics SDKs (beyond the basic Unity SDK diagnostics described in section 1.2a).
- Crash-reporting services.
- Social-network sign-in.
- Push notifications.

## 2. How we use the information

The data above is used only to:

1. Sign you in anonymously and run a multiplayer match between two players (matchmaking + peer-to-peer relay).
2. Display each player's chosen name and avatar inside the game.
3. Maintain the competitive rank ladder (your skill rating and the standings shown on the leaderboard) for online matches, including verifying ranked results to prevent cheating.
4. Save your local preferences and progress so the app behaves the same the next time you open it.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

## 4. Data retention

- **On your device:** local preferences and progress persist until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
- **Unity Lobby data:** automatically expires shortly after a match ends (Unity deletes inactive lobbies within minutes).
- **Unity Relay session data:** discarded by Unity when the match disconnects.
- **Anonymous player ID and installation ID:** kept by Unity for as long as your anonymous Unity account exists, until you request deletion (see section 6) or Unity removes inactive accounts under its own retention policy.
- **Competitive leaderboard entry:** your rating and display name persist on Unity's Leaderboards service until you request deletion of your anonymous Unity account (see section 6) or Unity removes the data.

We do not keep any database of players on our own systems; the leaderboard is hosted by Unity Gaming Services.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at the email above and we will delete any such information.

## 6. Your rights

Because we do not store personal data on our own systems, there is generally nothing for us to delete on request. You can:

- **Reset local data:** Settings → Apps → QuizConquest → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
- **Limit online data:** play only the single-player, hot-seat, or Daily Challenge modes. These never contact the leaderboard or matchmaking services. (The anonymous sign-in at launch described in section 1.2 still happens. To avoid it completely, play with your device offline.)
- **Request deletion of your data:** email us at lostfrogs2026@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". Open the game's leaderboard and include the display name you used there so we can find your entry. We will delete your anonymous Unity player account, which removes your player ID and your competitive leaderboard entry, within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also make a request directly to Unity under Unity's privacy policy linked above.

If you have any other request, email us at lostfrogs2026@gmail.com.

## 7. Security

All data sent to Unity Gaming Services and Google Play is **encrypted in transit** (HTTPS/TLS, and DTLS for relayed match traffic). The app blocks cleartext network traffic (`android:usesCleartextTraffic="false"`).
